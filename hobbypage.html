My Hobbies


Going On Nature Walks

Ever since I graduated high school, I have been enjoying going out into nature and just briefly disconnecting from my busy life. These walks are very theraputic for me as I get to enjoy the soothing sounds of nature and take in fresh air. I also get in some leg exercise during these walks as the trails I like the most are the longer ones (2-6 miles). Lastly, these walks also made me aware of the nature around me. I did not know so many species of plants and animals live in Florida until I did these walks and it is very fascinating. I also got to learn of the hardships that they face due to climate change, conservation laws, etc. Overall, I encourage you all to take a nature walk if you have space in your schedule.
